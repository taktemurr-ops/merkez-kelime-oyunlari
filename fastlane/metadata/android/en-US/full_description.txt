Word Games brings 34 word games together in one app with our own original design. Grids and crosswords, find and build, guessing, speed, meaning and rival games; every game keeps its own progress, missions and achievements. Words come from open-licensed lists in 18 languages; clue-based games are played in English and Turkish.

• Grids and crosswords: Crossword, Arrow Puzzle, Codeword, Cryptogram, Word Sudoku, Word Square, Acrostic
• Find and build: Word Hunt, Letter Wheel, Word Heap, Letter Hive, Letter Grid, Word Blocks, Word Pieces, Missing Letters, Letter Box, Theme Hunt
• Guessing: Five Letters, Word Guess Collection (8), Petal Guess, Word Ladder, Letter Slide, Four Pictures One Word
• Speed and reflex: Speed Typing, Falling Letters, Anagram Race
• Meaning and language: Same & Opposite, Four Groups, Proverb Fill, Spell It Right, Five Clues
• Against a rival: Word Board, Word Duel, Word Chain, Words & Sums

Highlights
• 100,000 levels per game; words come from open-licensed lists in 18 languages with inappropriate words filtered out. The word language can be chosen separately from the app language.
• Ten difficulty steps: relaxed early levels, expert challenges later.
• Hints reveal letters, remove wrong options or show the next word.
• Daily and weekly levels, missions, achievements and 33 languages.
• Plays offline on phones, tablets and the web.
• No gambling, betting or 18+ content; suitable for all ages.
• 🧩 Puzzle of the Day: Everyone gets the same puzzle today. How fast are you? 🔥 · Today's leaderboard · Share result
• 🏆 Family Tournament: 2–8 players on one device, take turns!

Purchases (such as removing ads) apply to every game in the app.
